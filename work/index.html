Builds

Publicis Warehouse

A client commissioned me to solve one problem, and it turned into a product that’s still running.

idea to prototype: 4 weeks · prototype code: mine · status: live

Warehouse connects retailer data to a brand’s first-party data and builds pseudonymous segments: shoppers who have bought the product, and shoppers who are in market for it. Those segments are activated as targeting audiences. Scaling it from one client’s prototype into a product was the harder build.

Clean-room framework

I built the framework that lets a brand and a retailer work on each other’s data while each keeps control of its own.

A data clean room lets a brand and a retailer match first-party data sets and query the overlap under agreed rules, so neither side sees the other’s row-level records. The framework sets the match keys and the queries each side is allowed to run.

Save Pub Life

Save Pub Life launched as a working marketplace in three weeks.

B2B2C marketplace · live in 3 weeks · 500+ pubs at launch

A B2B2C marketplace serves two sets of customers through one platform: the pubs as trade customers, and the people who buy from those pubs. Both sides had to be live at launch.
